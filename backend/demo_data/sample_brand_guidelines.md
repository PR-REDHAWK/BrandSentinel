# Acme Global Brand Guidelines & Communications Manual (v3.0)

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity
Acme Corporation is an enterprise cloud and brand intelligence provider. Our brand reputation relies on uncompromising factual accuracy, technological credibility, and ethical governance.

## 2. Core Communication Principles
- **Evidence-Based Authority:** Every numerical assertion, benchmark, or efficiency metric must cite an audited source.
- **Tone:** Confident, objective, and consultative. Avoid sensationalism, colloquial slang, and emotional hype.
- **AI Suggests, Humans Decide:** Machine intelligence assists our review teams; humans maintain final authority and editorial accountability.

## 3. Approved Enterprise Facts & Verifications
- **Cloud Infrastructure Savings:** "Customers achieve up to 30% reduction in infrastructure costs within six months." (Source: Q4 2025 Auditor Report, p. 12).
- **Service Level Agreement:** "BrandSentinel guarantees 99.99% platform availability backed by an audited enterprise SLA." (Source: Enterprise Master Service Agreement v3.2).
- **Compliance Certification:** "SOC-2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA certified for secure data processing." (Source: Bureau Veritas Security Audit 2025).

## 4. Banned Vocabulary & Prohibited Jargon
The following terms are strictly prohibited in all customer-facing materials:
- `dirt cheap`: Colloquial slang that devalues enterprise engineering rigor. Use **cost-effective** instead.
- `crushes the competition`: Aggressive, unsubstantiated competitive comparison. Use **outperforms industry benchmarks** instead.
- `foolproof`: Absolute guarantee that exposes the company to legal liability. Use **resilient** or **fault-tolerant** instead.
- `miracle fix`: Pseudoscientific hyperbole. Use **engineered solution** instead.

## 5. Visual Standards & Accessibility Requirements
- **Primary Brand Navy:** `#0B2046`
- **Accent Emerald:** `#10B981`
- **Background White:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Logo Proportions:** The official horizontal logo must maintain a 3.5:1 aspect ratio. Stretched, compressed, or distorted logos are strictly prohibited.
- **WCAG 2.2 Level AA Contrast:** All text content must achieve a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its background.
- **Alternative Text:** All embedded images and infographics must have descriptive alternative text (`alt`).
